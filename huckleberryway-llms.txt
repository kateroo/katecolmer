# Huckleberry Way

> Huckleberry Way runs a system of authority that gives AI depth and certainty about your business, so that when a buyer asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Google who to call, the answer is you. At its centre is your public system of record: The Authority Record™, one verified master record of who you are, what you do and what you have proven, kept true and published everywhere AI looks. You own the record. We run the system.

AI recommends the businesses it can confidently resolve as one entity and verify at the source. Huckleberry Way builds verified entity architecture for Canadian businesses; the structured identity layer that determines whether AI systems find you, verify you, cite you, and recommend you with confidence.

**How the system wins the recommendation.** Before an AI assistant names a business, it has to find it, understand it and verify it. Huckleberry Way's system of authority does all three, as one continuous system rather than a set of separate services:
- **Identity engineering** makes the business one entity everywhere it appears: one name, one set of facts, one identity for every location, reconciled across its website, its Google, Apple and Bing profiles and the knowledge graphs. This is what lets AI find and resolve it.
- **Relevance engineering** connects the business's services, credentials, people, places and outcomes as explicit relationships, so AI can reason about it and match it to the question a buyer actually asks, not only to its name.
- **Proof engineering** turns the business's results into evidence a machine can verify and cite: customer stories, case studies, credentials and outcomes, each tied to the exact service, person and location it proves.
- **Structured Posts** carry the Record outward on a schedule. Each post is written as a subject-predicate-object statement, the shape of an RDF triple: one falsifiable fact with the evidence behind it. They are published to the Google Business Profile, social channels, website and feed, so every surface states the same facts (semantic coherence) and independent surfaces corroborate one another.
- **Smart Reviews** keeps reputation signals fresh: real customers invited in the business's own name, and every review answered in its voice.

All of it is written into one verified master record, The Authority Record™, and kept current by Record Governance, so nothing drifts as the business changes. Identity lets AI resolve the business, relationships let it reason about the business, and evidence lets it verify what the business claims: the three things AI needs before it recommends anyone.

## 6. How AI decides: the frameworks behind the work
- **The two gates:** Gate one is retrieval: can it find you when it goes looking? The verdict is considered, or invisible, and it is decided by identity: whether you resolve as one thing, described the same way everywhere. Gate two is reasoning: can it understand you well enough to name you? The verdict is named, or merely present, and it is decided by evidence: whether what you claim connects, agrees across sources, and traces to someone who is not you. Ranking gets you considered; verification gets you recommended. Full treatment: https://www.huckleberryway.ca/how-it-works
- **The seven checks:** (1) Legible: your facts exist in a form machines parse, not prose they guess at. (2) Resolvable: you are one distinct entity everywhere you appear. (3) Verifiable: every claim is tied to the source that verifies it. (4) Corroborated: independent sources agree about you; agreement is how machines decide what is true. (5) Comprehensible: your services, credentials, positions and outcomes connect, so a system can reason over you, not just retrieve you. (6) Proven: your results exist as evidence a machine can cite, not adjectives it must discount. (7) Current: the record moves as your business moves. Legible and Resolvable are built at Found, Comprehensible at Understood, Proven at Proven; Verifiable and Corroborated run through every stage; Current is Kept.
- **Extraction versus declaration:** A fact pulled from prose is inferred, and inference can misread or invent a connection that was never there. A fact declared as subject, relationship, object and source is asserted once, correctly, and every system downstream reads the same thing instead of guessing it again. A business expressed as connected facts can be reasoned about; a business expressed as prose can only be quoted.
- **Not all verification is equal:** A fact verified by the body that conferred it outranks one repeated by a site that merely hosts it, which outranks anything a business says about itself. The ranking follows who could revoke the fact: a registry can withdraw a registration and a certifying body can withdraw a certification.
- **Relevance engineering:** Building a machine-readable business identity with enough depth, breadth and fidelity that it holds up under verification from many sources, by any agentic system, in any context.
- **Coherence and uniqueness:** Coherence (consistent, connected declarations across systems) makes AI treat facts as settled; uniqueness (documented outcomes no one else can claim) makes AI cite a business by name. Coherence without uniqueness is recognized but interchangeable; uniqueness without coherence is novel but unverifiable. Both are required.
- **Zero Drift:** Identical facts across every surface, not similar ones: the operational standard Record Governance maintains.
- **The Story Bible:** The machine-readable account of a business that ensures every AI system meets the same canonical facts. Not a brand guidelines document: a structured, declarative record machines can verify without interpretation.
